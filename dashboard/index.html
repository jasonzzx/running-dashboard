How this distribution is calculated

Each interval between consecutive original samples is assigned to a 30-second pace range using its distance increase and elapsed time. Time and distance are summed separately; these are not activity-average paces or counts of samples. Ranges include their lower bound and exclude their upper bound. The first and last ranges include all faster and slower valid paces.

We exclude recorded timer stops, stationary or backwards-distance intervals, missing values, gaps longer than 30 seconds, and speeds above 10 m/s. Where timer events are absent, the distance and gap checks still apply. Short unmarked pauses can remain. Excluded intervals are reported separately and are not assigned a pace. Totals may differ from Strava summaries. Pace is an estimate from recorded distance; GPS errors and hills still affect it.
